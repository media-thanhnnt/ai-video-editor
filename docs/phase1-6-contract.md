# Phase 1.6 — Per-scene Voice Engine Contract

## Purpose and scope

Build `final.mp4` with accurately placed per-scene narration, reusing `output/video_only.mp4` from Phase 1.5. No new video frames are rendered by the `audio` command. The video input remains H.264/yuv420p/BT.709 and is stream-copied to the final MP4. Source narration may be MP3 or WAV.

Music, ducking, LUFS normalization, single-track alignment and subtitle burn-in belong to Phase 3, not Phase 1.6.

## CLI

```powershell
ai-video-editor audio phase16_demo
```

Requires `editing_plan.json`, `timeline.json`, valid media and `output/video_only.mp4` generated by Phase 1.5.

## Input invariants

- `timeline.json` is validated against current assets, storyboard and plan. If hashes/timestamps differ, the command exits before publishing an output.
- Every voice segment has a unique `scene_id`, a non-negative sample position, and a strictly positive duration.
- No voice segments overlap, and no voice extends beyond `total_samples`.
- Every input file is resolved and validated as a project-local path.
- MP3 and WAV inputs are decoded to 16-bit PCM at the configured sample rate and output channel count; sample count must **exactly** match the timeline. There is no time-stretching or silent trimming.
- Source video must have precisely `total_frames` and must match resolution, FPS, codec, pixel format and BT.709 metadata.

## Outputs

- `work/audio/voice_mix.wav`: PCM16, deterministic silence gaps, 1 or 2 channels.
- `output/final.mp4`: MP4, H.264 video stream copy + AAC-LC 192 kbps audio.
- `reports/audio_report.json`: source video inspection, PCM placements, MP4 stream QA, AAC sample difference and inherited warnings.
- Final output is staged as `final.partial.mp4` and renamed only after technical QA succeeds.

## MP4/AAC acceptance

- Exactly 1 video track and 1 audio track.
- Frame count and resolution must exactly match the saved timeline.
- Audio codec AAC LC with the correct sample rate and channels.
- AAC decoded duration may differ from PCM by up to 2048 samples (encoder padding/priming). Report actual sample delta.
- Verify playback and subjective voice quality on Windows before accepting.

## Known limitations

- Sample-based synchronization assumes media metadata from Phase 1.2 was calculated using a compatible decoding path. A new/different decoder may produce a count mismatch for some MP3s; report and revalidate rather than silently alter the audio.
- Audio clipping and loudness compliance are *not* guaranteed until Phase 3.
- A video with matching frames but visual content created under a different plan may not be detected without a video provenance key. Avoid replacing `video_only.mp4` manually between render and mux.
- Full audio mix is generated on disk; the implementation does not yet support pause/resume during individual FFmpeg decode/encode operations.
